## 🎬 Act 1: The Core Pipeline (00:00 – 00:25)
*Goal: Trigger the initial workflow diagram (`flow (v1)`) and automatic step illustrations.*

**Alex**: "Good morning everyone. Thanks for dialing in. Today we need to lock in the architecture for our next-generation automated document processing pipeline. Sarah, can you walk us through the high-level stages from intake to delivery?"

**Sarah**: "Certainly, Alex. Here is the operational sequence:
First we capture images as initial step, then we convert images to text where captured images are processed and text extracted, then we do internal review where an internal review is performed before release, then we share text with client."

> 💡 **What AEGIS does**:
> - Renders a sleek OLED flow diagram card in the canvas.
> - Creates top tab: **`[📋 Document Processing (v1)]`**.
> - Shows cyan neon connector arrows with flowing energy particles.

**Ken**: "Sarah, the text conversion and OCR extraction phase is quite intricate for non-engineers. Can we illustrate that step visually so stakeholders can see how the extraction works?"

**Sarah**: "Illustrate convert images to text with Nano Banana"

> 💡 **What AEGIS does**:
> - Invokes `gemini-2.5-flash-image` (Nano Banana).
> - Generates a new dedicated top tab: **`[🖼️ Step 2: Convert images to text]`**.
> - Displays a high-contrast OCR document scanner illustration with a 1-sentence technical caption below it.

## 🎬 Act 2: Architecture Expansion & Database Weigh-In (00:25 – 00:50)
*Goal: Demonstrate diagram versioning (creating `(v2)` without overwriting `(v1)`) and generate a comparison table (`compare`).*

**Elena**: "Looking at the initial flow, we are missing a critical compliance step. Before any internal review, we are legally required by European partners to validate GDPR compliance and redact customer PII."

**Alex**: "Great catch Elena. Let's update our pipeline: Add legal compliance check after convert images to text."

> 💡 **What AEGIS does**:
> - Pulls the existing diagram structure from `(v1)`.
> - Inserts the legal compliance check node.
> - Creates a **NEW tab**: **`[📋 Document Processing (v2)]`**.
> - **Past history preserved**: You can click `(v1)` to view the original 4 steps, or `(v2)` to view the updated 5-step pipeline!

**Ken**: "Next agenda item: we need to decide on our metadata storage engine. We are currently weighing Cloud Firestore versus Cloud Spanner."

**Sarah**: "Let's compare them:
For Firestore, cost is low, latency is sub-10 milliseconds, multi-region replication is standard, but complex SQL joins are limited.
For Cloud Spanner, cost is high, latency is 5 to 15 milliseconds, it provides full relational SQL with external consistency, but requires provisioned node overhead."

> 💡 **What AEGIS does**:
> - Autonomously generates a **Comparison Table (`compare`)**.
> - Renders formatted columns for Firestore vs Cloud Spanner across Cost, Latency, Consistency, and Scaling.
> - Every cell has an interactive `data-ref` (clicking any cell drills into that metric).
